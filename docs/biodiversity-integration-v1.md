## Evidence separation
1. Taxonomic reference: retain source identifiers and original names from ecological evidence providers; do not add a botanical learner catalog or identification interface.
2. Regional occurrence: taxon ID, geographic scope, date/filter, provider, retrieval completeness, provisional ID status; counts are not abundance or absence.
3. Nativity: county FIPS, taxon concept, BONAP/NAPA evidence and date, review status; distinguish confirmed local, nearby native, introduced and unknown. Do not infer from occurrence.
4. Potential relationships: original source taxon and plant rank, qualification, source row/ID, citations, scope; genus association does not establish all species are hosts. Distinguish observed interactions from potential.
5. Field record: property/zone ID, observation date, taxon, verification, observer notes and optional local media; private export only. Not implemented in this increment.
6. Recommendations: explicit habitat compatibility and evidence, no arbitrary score, no recommendation from catalog alone. Unknowns remain null.
