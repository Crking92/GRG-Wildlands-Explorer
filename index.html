Method & limitations

Query all eleven TPWD regional polygon layers using the boundary's bounding box. Intersect features with the full study geometry, dissolve within class, check overlap, calculate geodesic areas using Turf and report the remainder. Small geometry discrepancies are disclosed. Original map objects were generated at 10 m resolution; results are not proof of current native cover, individual species or restoration suitability. Requests send a bounding rectangle to TPWD. Alpha limit: 1,000 acres, 500 source polygons and 300,000 vertices; subdivide larger or complex sites.

Data sources and limitations

USGS National Map: aerial imagery is historical orthophotography, not live satellite footage; topography is a composite reference map.

TPWD Ecological Mapping Systems: modeled vegetation communities, not field-confirmed native cover.

USGS Elevation Point Query Service: interpolated elevation. Accuracy varies.

External services receive tile and point requests. Boundaries stay in your browser unless exported. No accounts, analytics, paid services or game achievements.
