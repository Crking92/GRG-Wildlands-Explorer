Method & limitations

Query all eleven TPWD regional polygon layers using the boundary's bounding box. Intersect features with the full study geometry, dissolve within class, check overlap, calculate geodesic areas using Turf and report the remainder. Small geometry discrepancies are disclosed. Original map objects were generated at 10 m resolution; results are not proof of current native cover, individual species or restoration suitability. Requests send a bounding rectangle to TPWD. Alpha limit: 1,000 acres, 500 source polygons and 300,000 vertices; subdivide larger or complex sites.

Profile method & limitations

USGS 3DEP raw elevation samples, up to 401 points along a straight geodesic line of 1–5,000 m. No interpolation across missing data. Projected grid and sample spacing do not establish source LiDAR resolution. Sample extrema are not surveyed elevations. Only the current profile is retained; export before replacing it. Profile coordinates are sent to the public USGS service as a bounding-box request.

Source & limitations

Adaptive floating-point GeoTIFF elevation grid in Web Mercator, ground-area weighted. Grid spacing is requested, not actual local DEM resolution. Local source resolution, date and vertical datum not verified. Slope and aspect statistics are derived from a padded 3 × 3 neighborhood of raw elevations with local ground-spacing correction. Planning slope classes and the nearly-flat threshold are not engineering standards. Contours are visualization, not surveyed grades.

Select a study area.

Method & uncertainty

Unconditioned D8-style routing from USGS 3DEP. Strictly downhill routes; ties, flats and low cells can stop flow. Accumulation includes the receiving cell and only terrain within the requested rectangle. The rectangular boundary is not a watershed. No sink filling, culvert routing, storm drains, rainfall, soil infiltration or hydraulic simulation. Unknown upstream terrain can change results. Low cells are not confirmed ponds. The projected grid is not native LiDAR resolution. Independent GIS and field validation pending.
